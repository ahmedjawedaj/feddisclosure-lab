# v0.15 pre-registration

Written 2026-11-23 before any v0.15 experiment was run. The commit that adds this file is the timestamp. Local runs carry no timestamps, so their order relative to this file rests on commit order. Every prediction below has a numeric pass rule. A prediction that fails is recorded as FAIL in the results and is not reworded. Thresholds are never changed after the first run that uses them. If a threshold turns out to be badly chosen, the original stays, a deviation entry is added at the end of this file, and both outcomes are reported.

## Conventions

1. **Seeds.** Evaluation seeds are 0 to N-1. Tuning seeds (attack iterations, learning rates, anything chosen by looking at results) are 9000 and up (`feddisclosure.evaluation.intervals.tuning_seed`). A number reported as a result never comes from a tuning seed.
2. **Intervals.** Rates use Wilson 95% intervals. Any comparison of an attack rate with a formal bound uses Clopper-Pearson 95% intervals. Continuous metrics use a percentile bootstrap over seeds with at least 2,000 resamples.
3. **Baselines.** Every inversion number is reported next to the label-conditional floor (class-average image of the public pool, labels known to the attacker). "Recognisable" means per-image MSE below 0.05, as in v0.14.2.
4. **Labels.** Predictions written after a feasibility probe are marked `[post-probe]`. They are weaker evidence than predictions written blind.
5. **Failure handling.** A gate breach (for example an attack beating a formal bound) is treated as a bug in the mechanism or the accounting. It stops the phase until the cause is found.
6. **Disclosure.** Any finding about third-party software is reported privately to its maintainers before it is described in a public commit, issue, release note or report.
7. **Authorship.** All commits from fe83f8b (v0.14) onward are authored under the name Ahmed Jawed with no trailers for any AI tool. Merge commits made on GitHub use the account's noreply address. The 17 earlier commits (c46bde6 to 6fa04ca, v0.4.1 to v0.13.0) carry project identities.

## DP bound against an informed attacker (WP5)

Setup: real Opacus, one full-batch step, clip 1.0, noise multiplier sigma, 8 records per client, 1,000 trials per cell, attacker knows the other records and picks among u candidates (Hayes, Mahloujifar and Balle, Algorithm 2).

| ID | Prediction | Pass rule |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | No cell breaks the bound | For every cell, the Clopper-Pearson lower limit of attack success is at most the bound plus 0.01. A breach is a gate failure |
| D2 `[post-probe]` | The attack is far above chance at sigma 0.2, u 10 | Wilson lower limit above 0.60 |
| D3 `[post-probe]` | The attack is near the bound at high noise | At sigma 4, u 10 the attack rate is within 0.05 of the bound |
| D4 | Success falls as sigma grows | Attack rate at sigma 4 is below the rate at sigma 0.5 for every u, with non-overlapping Wilson intervals |
| D5 | The exact optimal success for the real candidate set is at least the attack success and at most the bound | Holds in every cell up to Monte Carlo error (Clopper-Pearson) |

## Deviation log and interpretation notes

No threshold was changed. Notes added after runs, each dated 2026-11-23. Entries 4 to 11 were added after a separate review pass (R2) of the results on main. Entries 12 to 21 were added after a further review (R3) and cover the S1, S2, S3 and X1 work (WP9 and WP10). Each states the reading used, the verdict under the alternative reading where it is known, and that no threshold changed.

1. **D3 wording.** The row does not say whether "u 10" means same-label or any-label candidates. The analysis script requires both modes to pass. That rule is recorded in the script and results commit (c73dcb9) and was not separately timestamped before the run. Result: any-label passes (gap 0.022), same-label fails (gap 0.055 against the 0.05 limit). D3 is recorded as FAIL. The same-label attack is 0.022 below the exact optimum for its own candidate sets, which is about 2.2 standard errors at 1,000 trials (paired z of -2.17), so it is on the large side for sampling noise alone. No threshold changed.
2. **P2 configuration.** The replay study had no saved native 4-client run to start from, so P2 was judged in replay on the shipped config `fedavg_digits_iid_4clients.yaml` (2 rounds), chosen before seeing results. Upstream pass rate over the 24 reply orders is 100 percent, so P2 is recorded as FAIL. An exploratory 4-client MNIST 10-round replay gave 83.3 percent (20 of 24), and 3-client native replays gave 0 percent for two runs. These exploratory numbers are reported but do not change the verdict. Correction: live run 45076810526 has since produced native 4-client runs (Digits iid, 10 rounds, upstream and deterministic aggregation). Each has a single reply order, passes the gate upstream and cannot show order dependence, so it does not change the P2 verdict. No threshold changed.
3. **E2.** Neither clause held. The float32 path adds a deterministic bias of up to 3/16 of a quantisation unit per client but never breaks the float64-only bound 2cn/T. Recorded as FAIL.
4. **D2 and D4 operational rules.** D2 does not say which candidate mode "u 10" means, and D4 does not say which modes "every u" covers. D2 was read as needing both candidate modes at u 10 (same-label and any-label), and D4 as needing all five candidate sets (same-label u 10 and 100, any-label u 10, 100 and 1000) to show the fall with sigma. Both rules are recorded in the script and results commit and were not separately timestamped before the run. Both checks pass, and they also pass under every narrower reading (D2 same-label alone has a Wilson lower limit of 0.632 and any-label alone 0.926, D4 passes for each set separately). No verdict changes with the reading. No threshold changed.
5. **D5 Monte Carlo error.** "Up to Monte Carlo error" was read as an overlap rule: the Clopper-Pearson lower limit of the exact optimum is at most the bound, and the lower limit of each attack rate is at most the upper limit of the optimum. Read strictly on point estimates, the attack is at or below the optimum in 18 of 25 cells and the optimum is at or below the bound in all 25. The 7 other cells have attack points slightly above the optimum point. A strict paired test also passes: the paired z of the attack against the optimum on the same trials has a maximum of +0.77, so no cell has an attack significantly above its optimum. The mean paired z is -0.52 and three cells are below -2 (any-label u 100 at sigma 0.2 and 0.5, same-label u 10 at sigma 4), which is a mild shortfall of the attack that is not explained. No threshold changed.
6. **W3 pooled reading.** W3 does not say whether the tolerances apply per seed or pooled. They were applied to pooled rates and mean MSE over the 5 seeds. The per-seed gaps (captured minus simulated) are +0.25, 0, -0.125, -0.25 and +0.25 in rate and -0.0111, -0.0013, +0.0100, +0.0154 and +0.0011 in MSE. They cancel in the pooled figures (2.5 points and 0.0028). Under a per-seed reading four seeds exceed the 10 point tolerance and two exceed 0.01 in MSE, so W3 would FAIL. Five seeds give low power, and the verdict is labelled PASS (pooled, low power). No threshold changed.
7. **W5 passes on sample counts only.** W5 names counts and metrics. The repository client returns no metric values, so the metrics half was vacuous: the metrics record was present in 20 of 20 replies and empty in all of them. W5 passes on sample counts (20 of 20 visible and correct). Under a reading that needs observed metric values the row could not be tested. The label is PASS for sample counts, metrics not exercised. No threshold changed.
8. **P1 scope.** P1 says native runs. It was judged in replay on the 7 saved native runs, plus 3 live Digits configs (6 hosted runs with the strategy on and off). The live configs do not include a config that fails upstream, because the MNIST configs that failed upstream were not run live. The strategy is the reference arithmetic by construction. P1 is recorded as PASS in replay only. The live runs confirm transport and client determinism. Under a reading that needs the 7 native runs re-run live with the strategy on, P1 is untested and not a pass. No threshold changed.
9. **F1 fault set and verdict split.** The row says "every seeded fault is caught by at least one detector except possibly mask reuse". Mask reuse was outside the scope of the work package, so it was not seeded. The five faults are DP noise scale, skipped clipping, plaintext fallback, threshold and dropout handling, and a stale global model. The plaintext fallback and the stale model stand in for the mask reuse slot, so the fault set differs from the registered wording. The choice to give the verdict on the strict count (4 of 5 by detectors A, B, C and D1 as specified) and to report the D2 extension (5 of 5) separately was recorded in the script and results commit and not before the run, so it is treated as chosen after the fact. The numeric rule passes under either count. The prediction text is partly falsified for F3 (plaintext fallback), which no specified detector catches. F4a and F4b test how the verifiers read tampered evidence, not a deployed stack. No threshold changed.
10. **F2 detector.** F2 says "many-run DP audit". The white-box chi-square audit (ten real Opacus runs per audit, residual against the known clipped sum) was used as the many-run audit. It has power 1.000 at noise factors 0.25, 0.5 and 2 with false positive rate 0.010, so F2 passes. The black-box informed-attack detector, which needs only the update, reaches 0.95 at factor 0.5, 1.000 at 0.25 and 0 at factor 2. Under a black-box reading F2 would FAIL, because extra noise is invisible to it. "A factor of 2 or more" was read as including 0.25 and 0.5 (noise too low) as well as 2 (noise too high). No threshold changed.
11. **B1 headline configuration.** The headline external configuration is the tuned breaching configuration (`tuned_ig_tv0.001`, total variation scale chosen on tuning seeds 9000 to 9002). That choice is written in `scripts/run_external_baselines.py` and was decided before the evaluation seeds were run, but it is not timestamped separately from the results commit. B1 is FAIL against it (ratio 1.86, bootstrap 95 percent interval 1.47 to 2.51 over seeds). Against the two shipped library configurations B1 would PASS (ratios 0.24 and 0.47). The repo attack was not tuned, so the comparison favours breaching. No threshold changed.

12. **X1 and S1 and S2 primary `max_weight` of 1000.** The pre-registration does not name a SecAgg+ `max_weight`. The primary value is 1000, which is the upstream default and the setting in the README headline. `tune_one` hardcodes it. WP9 and WP10 landed in one commit (d328fa3), so the claim that 1000 was fixed before the runs is not separately timestamped. The choice hurts X1. At `max_weight` 16 the X1 collusion part passes (+13.7 points against the +10 rule), at 1000 it fails (+9.4). The verdict is recorded at 1000 and the 16 result is reported next to it. S1 and S2 pass at both settings (S1 aggregate gap -39.6 at 1000 and -35.0 at 16, S2 collusion 90.8% at 1000 and 95.8% at 16, both at 8 clients). No threshold changed.
13. **S1, S2 and X1 are judged on pooled point estimates.** The rules do not say whether to judge a point estimate or an interval limit. They were applied to the pooled recognisable rates, as C2 and C3 were. Images of one seed are not independent, so the seed-level bootstrap (4,000 resamples for S1 and S2, 10,000 for the X1 gap) is shown next to every point estimate. S1 passes under the bootstrap too (gap -39.6, interval -47.5 to -32.1). S2 passes on the Wilson lower limits (86.0% and 86.5%) and on the seed-bootstrap lower limits (85.8% at 4 clients and 86.2% at 8). X1 does not settle under the bootstrap. The collusion gap is +9.4 points with an interval of +0.6 to +18.8, so the interval is too wide to settle it (see entry 21). No threshold changed.
14. **S1 is read one-sided and passes vacuously.** The prediction says the aggregate view "stays at the floor". The rule says "at most floor plus 5 points", which a very weak attack meets by being far below the floor. The aggregate is 39.6 points below the floor at 8 clients. A two-sided reading (within 5 points of the floor either way) would FAIL S1. The registered rule is one-sided and S1 is recorded as PASS, but it is a vacuous pass. It says the aggregate attack is weak, not that the aggregate is private, and it is not privacy evidence. Statements that the aggregate sits at or below the floor are conditional on this attack. No threshold changed.
15. **S2 plaintext level.** The prediction says collusion "stays at plaintext level". The rule is an absolute 85%, and that is what was applied, at both 4 and 8 clients. Collusion is 90.4% and 90.8% and plaintext is 95.0%, so the gap to plaintext is 4.6 and 4.2 points. No relative threshold was registered, so there is no alternative verdict to report. No threshold changed.
16. **S3 memory is summed over containers.** The rule says "peak container memory". It was read as the peak of the sum over all containers at one poll, which is the stricter reading. The summed peak is 3.25 GB against 6 GB. The largest single container peaked at 0.32 GB, so the per-container reading passes by a wider margin. No threshold changed.
17. **X1 verdict is the local 20-seed result.** The verdict comes from the local run on evaluation seeds 0 to 19 and is FAIL. The hosted Fashion-MNIST workflow has not been run. If it is run, it is a robustness check with its own tuning and 30 seeds, and it cannot change the registered verdict. If its collusion gap lands above 10 points it is reported as a second observation, not as a pass. No threshold changed.
18. **Tuning criterion.** The attack settings were chosen by lowest mean victim MSE on tuning seeds 9000 to 9002, not by recognisable rate. These can disagree. For the collusion view, 3,000 iterations and step 0.1, the recognisable rate on the 24 tuning images was 83.3% at total-variation weight 0.2 and 70.8% at 0.5, while the mean MSE was 0.0395 at 0.2 and 0.0382 at 0.5. The MSE criterion picked 0.5. A rate criterion would have picked 0.2. The evaluation was run with 0.5 only, so the X1 collusion rate under the other choice is unknown. No threshold changed.
19. **Tuning search was staged and stopped at an edge.** The search had up to four stages per view (a first grid, then iterations, then two extensions of the total-variation weight). An extension ran only when the best weight so far was the largest tried, so the range was widened after the first stage. The collusion weight of 0.5 is the largest weight tried. The step size of 0.1 is the lowest tried (grid 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3). 3,000 iterations is the highest count tried, and 3,000 was only tried with step 0.1. The aggregate view got 7 settings and the other two views 14 each, so the aggregate attack had a smaller search. A stronger collusion attack than the one evaluated may exist, which would raise the collusion gap. No threshold changed.
20. **Digits attacks are untuned.** The S1 and S2 digits attacks use 300 iterations (the default in `scripts/run_scaling_study.py`) and were not tuned. The Fashion-MNIST attacks were tuned (entries 18 and 19), so the two studies give the attacker different amounts of effort. No threshold changed.
21. **X1 is not settled by 20 seeds.** The collusion gap is +9.4 points against a +10 rule, with a seed bootstrap interval of +0.6 to +18.8 over 20 seeds (10,000 resamples). In that bootstrap the share of resamples with a gap of at least 10 points is about 0.475 (0.473 to 0.479 over three resampling seeds). The lower limit of the interval is +0.6, not 0.0. The computation is in `scripts/recompute_fashion_seed_bootstrap.py`, output `reports/results/privacy/v0150/fashion_mnist/seed_bootstrap.json`. The verdict is FAIL and is not reworded. A different seed set could well have passed. No threshold changed.
