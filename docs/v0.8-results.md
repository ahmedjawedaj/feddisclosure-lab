# v0.8 empirical results (not a privacy certification)

Run date: November 19, 2026. Approved public digits. Five sampling/initialization seeds 241–245, each three attack observations, 160 Adam attack steps. All 15 attacker/evaluator executions passed the integrity checker.

| Observer | Mean reconstruction MSE | Across-seed sample SD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Individual update | 0.01664135 | 0.01094022 |
| Aggregate-only target probe | 0.10847777 | 0.02153371 |
| Aggregate + updates of the other two clients | 0.01664135 | 0.01094022 |

Every collusion-isolated delta was tensor-close (max absolute discrepancy below 2e-6) to the original individual delta, as expected from the exact algebra and equal client weights. Larger MSE for aggregate-only is *specific to the deliberately misspecified target-only probe* and cannot establish protection against stronger attack optimization, additional auxiliary information, or side channels. Five seeds are exploratory; no hypothesis test was pre-registered. The attack uses the target labels as oracle side information. There is no SecAgg+ deployment or privacy-budget guarantee.

File locations: `reports/results/sprint4/aggregate_collusion/seed{241..245}/summary.json`, observation contracts, attacks and evaluator-only labels/images. Scalar summary and public-research figure: `reports/results/sprint4/aggregate_collusion/verification_summary.json` and `reports/figures/sprint4_aggregate_collusion.png`.
