# v0.16.8: other events, larger sizes, release values and values between 0 and gamma for NVIDIA FLARE's SVTPrivacy, measured with stated power

Status: measured on `nvflare` 2.9.1, integrated in release 0.16.8 (not yet peer reviewed).
Pre-registration: `docs/preregistration/v0.16-nvflare-events.md` (commit `a1d1ea5ecb8c774e68536b52d2d94596c56eb419`, committed alone before the script existed and before any event count from a real call of the filter in this phase).
Evidence: `reports/results/privacy/v0160/nvflare_events/nvflare_events.json`.
Script: `scripts/audit_nvflare_events.py`. Tests: `tests/privacy/test_audit_nvflare_events.py`.
Follows: `docs/v0.16-nvflare-selection-audit.md` (v0.16.5), whose limit 1 named what this release extends.

## Summary

v0.16.5 measured one event for one pair of inputs at small sizes. This release calls the real `SVTPrivacy.process_dxo` 6,000,000 times, 1,000,000 for each of six further configurations (500,000 per input, in 5 blocks of 100,000 with their own seeds). The configurations add larger d and n (d 10 to 40, n 4 to 10), the class default `replace` True, inputs with values between 0 and gamma, the release values and one event that joins the selection and the release. Each row has a claim that the documentation or the code implies, and the pre-registration states for every null result the smallest excess over the claim that the check would find with 80% power.

All 10 rows and all 83 scripted checks held. No lower bound exceeded its claim, so no replication was run. No prediction held only partly. Parts of the pre-registration that no script check covers are untested or read by hand: the probability of reporting an excess at the numeric truth, the chance of one block rejection among 12 worlds, the power of the block agreement test (taken from v0.16.5), the 1e-12 ledger tolerance (the script used 1e-9) and the eight items of the section "Not covered" of the pre-registration (other pairs, the marginal event, explicit release or threshold budgets, `replace` True in the release rows, larger sizes, composition and other versions).

1. The check can fail. At 500,000 runs per world the largest lower bound that any outcome can give is 11.64, far above every claim (the largest is 1.2).
2. Lower bounds on the epsilon of the event were 0.414, 0.206, 0.224 and 0.280 against a claim of 1 for the four selection rows (E1 to E4), 0.272 against a claim of 0.3 for the release row (E5) and 0.502 against a claim of 1.2 for the joint row (E6).
3. The smallest event epsilon that the check would have found with 80% power is 1.083, 1.063, 1.079 and 1.064 for the selection rows, 0.316 for the release row and 1.259 for the joint row. The excess over the claim is 8.3%, 6.3%, 7.9%, 6.4%, 5.5% and 4.9% of the claim. Every null result is informative by the pre-registered rule (excess at most half of the claim).
4. The release row is the closest to its claim. For two inputs that differ by a sign flip on every entry, the event "exactly n entries are nonzero and all are positive" has a log ratio of 0.2929 by the closed form, which is 0.976 of the claimed `epsilon_release` of 0.3, and the measured point estimate is 0.289 (0.962 of the claim). The lower bound is 0.272, which is 0.028 below the claim. In this configuration the release noise is calibrated to the claim for this event, with little room.
5. The numeric computation and the closed form predicted every proportion within 2.0 binomial standard errors (largest: E4 world a). The measured log ratios are 0.506, 0.280, 0.317, 0.353, 0.289 and 0.567 against 0.525, 0.302, 0.315, 0.330, 0.293 and 0.575 predicted, all inside 4 standard errors.
6. The whole script took 1,048.7 seconds on one core.

These are measurements of single events under single pairs of inputs in stated configurations. They are lower bounds on the epsilon of the filter and do not certify the claim for other events, pairs, sizes or parameter values.

## Setup

`nvflare` 2.9.1 in a separate virtual environment (Python 3.13.18, numpy 2.5.5, Linux, one core). The harness imports the filter and the DXO, Shareable and FLContext classes. No NVFlare server, client, job or training loop was started. The installed `svt_privacy.py` has sha256 `c543bbea2ce3bfe2e35a1d85a26acac8dffc0e95024e777010cdbf0cd0f92858`, the value of v0.16.5. The file is byte identical in 2.8.3, 2.9.0 and 2.9.1 (v0.16.5), so no other version was run.

Inputs are float64 vectors named `w` with gamma 1e-5. Other parameters are the class defaults, which includes `noise_var` 0.1 for the release. The claim of the selection rows is the ledger `epsilon_threshold` plus `epsilon_query` of the first call, which equals `epsilon` (1). The claim of E5 is the ledger `epsilon_release`, which is `n * noise_var` (0.3 at n 3). The claim of E6 is the ledger `epsilon_total` (1.2 at n 2).

| Row | d | n | `replace` | Worlds a and c (multiples of gamma) | Event | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | 10 | 4 | False | a: 1 on entries 0 to 3, else 0. c: reverse. | Nonzero entries are exactly entries 0 to 3. | 1 |
| E2 | 24 | 6 | True | a: 1 on entries 0 to 5, else 0. c: reverse. | At least 4 of entries 0 to 5 are nonzero. | 1 |
| E3 | 10 | 4 | False | a: 0.8 on entries 0 to 3, else 0.2. c: 0.2 on entries 0 to 3, else 0.8. | Nonzero entries are exactly entries 0 to 3. | 1 |
| E4 | 40 | 10 | False | a: 1 on entries 0 to 9, else 0. c: reverse. | At least 6 of entries 0 to 9 are nonzero. | 1 |
| E5 | 12 | 3 | False | a: +1 on all entries. c: -1 on all entries. | Exactly 3 entries are nonzero and all are positive. | 0.3 |
| E6 | 8 | 2 | False | a: +1 on entries 0 and 1, else 0. c: 0 on entries 0 and 1, else -1. | Entries 0 and 1 are positive and the rest are 0. | 1.2 |

Neighbouring inputs are not defined by the documentation or the code. Reading A: neighbours differ by at most gamma in every coordinate of the clipped absolute value. Reading B: they differ by at most gamma in the signed clipped value. E1 to E4 and E6 satisfy both readings (E3 differs by 0.6 gamma per coordinate, so it is less adverse by design). E5 satisfies reading A only, because a sign flip changes the signed value by 2 gamma. The release noise scale `2 * gamma * n / epsilon_release` is built for that range of 2 gamma per entry, which is why E5 uses reading A.

Block seeds are 20261201 + 10000 r + 100 w + b, with r the row number, w the world and b the block. A fresh filter instance is made every 10,000 calls. The interval is Clopper-Pearson with 1.25% in each tail, using the pure Python helper of `scripts/audit_nvflare_dp.py`. The lower bound is the largest of four log ratios (both directions, the event and its complement), as in v0.16.5.

The predictions come from a numeric computation that uses only the formulas of the acceptance loop and the selection step (no `nvflare` code, reproduced verbatim in the script) for E1 to E4 and for the selection part of E6, and from a closed form for the release part. It shares my reading of the filter, so it checks the filter against that reading and not against an independent source.

## Limits

1. Each row is one event and one pair. The exact set event could not be used at larger sizes, because its probability is about 1e-5 at d 24 and n 6, so E2 and E4 use "at least h of the first n entries are nonzero". The h was chosen from a numeric scan so that the event has probability near 0.005 to 0.01. The configurations are not a random sample.
2. The neighbouring definition is a reading, and E5 uses the reading that allows a sign flip. Under the signed reading, E5's pair is not a neighbouring pair.
3. Sizes stop at d 40 and n 10. The release rows use `replace` False and the default `noise_var`. No run used an explicit `epsilon_release`, `epsilon_threshold` or `epsilon_query`, `epsilon` other than 1, `gamma` or `tau` other than the defaults, several arrays, scalar passthrough or `NUM_STEPS_CURRENT_ROUND` other than 1. The full list is in the section "Not covered" of the pre-registration.
4. The Monte Carlo ran only on 2.9.1. The filter file is byte identical in 2.8.3, 2.9.0 and 2.9.1 (v0.16.5), which covers the code of this file and not the other dependencies of each release.
5. The numeric computation shares my reading of the acceptance loop and the selection step.
6. The lower bound is a statement about the event and pair. The power numbers use a normal approximation and the numeric denominator probability. The six rows are six separate checks with 5% family error each, and no correction was made across rows. If every claim held with the true epsilon at the numeric value, the chance of any row showing an excess is below 0.001.
7. A pass does not show that the accountant's sum (`pure_dp_sum` over calls) is right for composition, and no delta was considered.
8. The history cannot prove that no run happened before the commit order shows. The pre-registration commit was made before the script was written. Timing calls of the real filter (48,000 calls, times only) were made before the pre-registration and are listed in it.
9. The gate pins are not enforced in CI, as noted for v0.16.5.

## Reproduce

```bash
/path/to/venv/bin/python scripts/audit_nvflare_events.py            # about 17 minutes, writes the evidence file
/path/to/venv/bin/python scripts/audit_nvflare_events.py --quick --out out/   # about 35 seconds
/path/to/venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/privacy/test_audit_nvflare_events.py
```

Quick output is not evidence, and `--quick` requires `--out`. Script sha256 of the evidence run: `d94f24fda88fc3d14cd48e11dac77557817cca0ae25013d2eb1e8a68574591bf`.
